# AstroSage-Data-Analysis

This project is a data-driven business analysis of AstroSage (an online astrology/consultation platform), built around a hypothetical ₹1 Crore investment decision the company needs to make in its call center operations.

The core question: Should AstroSage invest in hiring more agents, improving training, or upgrading call center technology — and how should the ₹1 Cr be split across these to maximize efficiency, satisfaction, and profitability?

Dataset: A single flat operational log with 35 attributes covering ~8,365 calls and 28,027 chat/consultation records across 131 unique astrologers ("gurus") over a 34-day window (Dec 2023–Jan 2024) — including call/chat status, durations, ratings, revenue, and timestamps.

Deliverables in the package:

Objective questions: data structure, cleaning methodology, call volume trends, revenue by product/category, agent workload, correlation between call duration and satisfaction, top/bottom-rated gurus, categorical vs. continuous fields, etc.

Subjective/strategic questions: investment allocation reasoning, risk/mitigation matrices for hiring vs. training vs. technology, peak-hour handling strategy, workload-balancing recommendations, and a final justified breakdown of the ₹1 Cr budget (45% infrastructure, 25% dashboards/BI, 20% automated CX, 10% incentives).

Excel workbook — the underlying pivot-table/dashboard analysis (cleaned data, KPI calculations, correlation functions, interactive slicers) that the written and presentation answers are drawn from.
PowerPoint deck (17 slides) — an executive-style summary of the same findings:
Problem statement (3 investment options: Hire/Train/Upgrade Tech)
Data overview and methodology (Excel, pivot tables, COUNT/AVERAGE/SUM, CORREL, slicers)
Platform/channel distribution (Gurucool web 65%, app 33%, dashboard 2%)
Call and chat completion analysis (only 41% of calls, 28% of chats complete)
Rating distribution (average 2.93/8, large unrated pool)
Top-performing gurus and workload/burnout risk (some handling 50–76+ sessions/day)
Hourly demand patterns and peak-hour staffing needs
Revenue breakdown by consultation type (calls dominate at 78.7%)
Conclusions and final recommendations (a revised split of Technology 50% / Training 35% / Incentives 15%)
